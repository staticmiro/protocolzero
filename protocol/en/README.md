# Protocol Zero

**A comprehensive guide to digital privacy, security, and sovereignty for everyone.**

## What is Protocol Zero?

Protocol Zero is a practical manual for taking control of your digital life. No technical expertise required. Just clear explanations, actionable steps, and real protection.

**Protocol Zero** is a complete system for managing your digital life safely, privately, and sustainably. 

- **Universal:** For everyone, regardless of technical skill
- **Timeless:** Principles over tools—stays relevant as technology changes
- **Practical:** Actionable steps, not just theory
- **Free:** Always free, always accessible
- **Open:** Community-maintained, transparent versioning

**Total:** 52 Chapters across 12 Parts + 6 Appendices = Complete Digital Life System

## 🗺️ How to Read This Document

Protocol Zero is modular. You don't have to read it front to back.

### Roadmap by Available Time

**If you have 1 hour:**  
Read [Part II: Identity and Access](02_identity_and_access.md) (Ch 2-3). Set up a password manager and enable two-factor authentication on your most important accounts (email, bank).

**If you have 1 day:**  
Add [Part IV: Archive – Data & Memories](04_archive_data_and_memories.md) (Ch 12). Set up a basic backup system following the 3-2-1 rule. You'll protect yourself from device loss or failure.

**If you have 1 week:**  
Work through Parts II, III, IV, and V. You'll cover identity, device security, backups, and basic privacy. This is the core foundation that protects most people from most threats.

**If you have 1 month:**  
Read the entire document. Implement systematically. You'll build a complete digital life system that protects your data, your privacy, and your legacy.

**If you're in crisis:**  
Jump to [Appendix B: Crisis Scenarios](b_crisis_scenarios_first_aid.md). Find your situation. Follow the steps. Then come back and build prevention.

### Skill Levels

- **Beginner:** "I just don't want to lose my photos." → Start with [Part I](01_foundations.md), [Part II](02_identity_and_access.md), and [Part IV](04_archive_data_and_memories.md)
- **Intermediate:** "I want better privacy and security." → Add [Part III](03_device_security.md), [Part V](05_communication_and_privacy.md), and [Part VI](06_digital_finances.md)
- **Advanced:** "I want maximum control over my digital life." → Read everything, including optional advanced sections

### Content Structure

Each chapter follows this pattern:
- **Why it matters:** The reason you should care
- **What to do:** Specific, actionable steps
- **Common mistakes:** What people get wrong
- **Quick wins:** The fastest, highest-impact actions

**Priority Markers:**
- **[CRITICAL]** – Do this first. Non-negotiable for basic security
- **[RECOMMENDED]** – Significant improvement with reasonable effort
- **[ADVANCED]** – For those who want maximum control
- **[OPTIONAL]** – Specific to certain situations or threat models
